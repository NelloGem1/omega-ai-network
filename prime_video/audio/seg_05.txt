"The side-by-side test settled it. His ten numbers, checked one by one: three, five, seven, eleven, thirteen, seventeen, nineteen — seven true primes. But nine equals three times three, and fifteen equals three times five — impostors inside his own list. And the number two — the only even prime — was missing entirely. His list was simply all odd numbers from one to nineteen. Primes up to twenty happen to be mostly odd, so the superset caught them by coverage — not by discovery."
