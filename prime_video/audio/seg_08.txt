"And one level deeper — if two and five eliminate six endings, why not include three? The mod-ten law was really mod-thirty in disguise, because thirty equals two times three times five — it catches all the small prime divisors at once. The theorem: every prime greater than five lands in only eight residue classes modulo thirty — one, seven, eleven, thirteen, seventeen, nineteen, twenty-three, twenty-nine. Seventy-three percent of all numbers excluded. This is the actual sieve of Eratosthenes, formalized."
