"Then came the shock. In a twenty-by-twenty division table, Neilbert traced a diagonal where column plus row always equals nineteen. The cells produced clean consecutive odd numbers: one, three, five — all the way to nineteen. And when he added them all — one plus three plus five, up to nineteen — the sum was exactly one hundred. A perfect square. For a self-taught researcher, that felt like finding a hidden law of the universe."
