"Then came the insight that was really his. Neilbert had said the digits zero through nine are fundamental — everything past them is combinations. That's place value — the actual foundation of our number system. And a prime's last digit is its remainder mod ten. Check the endings: numbers ending in zero, two, four, six, or eight are even — divisible by two. Ending in five — divisible by five. Only one, three, seven, and nine survive. Every prime greater than five ends in one, three, seven, or nine. A necessary condition for primality — discovered from digit structure, exactly the way his tables suggested."
