"But when he tried to extend the table, the pattern seemed to hit a wall at column twenty-one. He concluded the primes stop there — that his list was complete. Devin tested it by running his exact table logic at sizes twenty, twenty-one, twenty-two, and one hundred. There is no wall. The boundary was a constant typed into the code — size equals twenty — not a law of nature. And reading the source revealed every cell was just the absolute difference of coordinates. Subtraction in disguise. True arithmetic — but it describes parity, not primality."
